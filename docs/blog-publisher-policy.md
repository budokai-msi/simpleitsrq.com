# Blog Publisher Policy

Hacker News is a discovery feed, not source material to copy.

Every automated post must:

- link to the original source and the Hacker News discussion;
- be original analysis written for Sarasota and Bradenton readers;
- avoid copying or lightly paraphrasing the source article;
- avoid invented customer stories, statistics, certifications, guarantees, or capabilities;
- keep Simple IT SRQ service claims limited to computer/hardware repair and diagnostics, business network and Microsoft 365 troubleshooting, and managed IT in the Sarasota-Bradenton area;
- treat Leadgen as a separate software product;
- use affiliate links only when the product is genuinely relevant to the article and disclose the relationship;
- use local SEO naturally, not by stuffing city names or unrelated service terms;
- prefer useful search intent such as computer repair Sarasota, computer diagnostics Bradenton, IT support Sarasota, business network help Bradenton, Microsoft 365 support Sarasota, and managed IT Sarasota/Bradenton.

Automated publication should fail closed when these rules cannot be verified.
