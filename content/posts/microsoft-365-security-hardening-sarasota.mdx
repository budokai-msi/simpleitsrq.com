## Short answer

Default Microsoft 365 settings leave key security gaps open. By hardening tenant policies, auditing mail flow rules, and requiring hardware MFA, you eliminate over 95% of business email compromise threats.

## Local impact for Sarasota & Bradenton businesses

Wire fraud and invoice manipulation target Florida real estate title companies, contractors, and professional service firms weekly. Standard passwords can be guessed or phished without MFA enforcing secure tokens.

When an attacker gains access to an unhardened 365 mailbox, they immediately set up hidden inbox forwarding rules to intercept invoices, banking instructions, and sensitive client correspondence.

## Recommended gear & solutions

- **Hardware Security Keys**: Require [[amazon:B07HBD71HL|YubiKey 5C NFC]] keys for all administrative and executive logins.
- **Enterprise Password Management**: Deploy 1Password or Bitwarden Teams for secure team credential sharing and vault auditing.

## What to do this week

1. Turn on Security Defaults or Conditional Access policies in Azure AD / Entra ID.
2. Disable IMAP, POP3, and legacy SMTP AUTH across all mailbox accounts.
3. Set up automated email alerts for external inbox forwarding rules and new admin assignments.
