## The Short Answer
A quality IT help desk for small business provides immediate, human responses and proactive maintenance to prevent problems before they stop your work. In the Sarasota area, this means having a technician who can be on-site quickly if a hardware failure happens, rather than someone working from a call center halfway across the globe. True support is measured by how quickly you get back to work, not by how many tickets a company closes in a month.

## Action Plan for This Week
If you are wondering if your current IT setup is actually serving you, take these four steps this week to audit your situation.

1. Test the response time. Send a non-urgent support request to your current provider on a Tuesday morning. See how long it takes for a human to actually respond. If it takes more than four hours, you have a problem.

2. Check your hardware age. Look at the stickers on your server or your main workstations. If they are more than five years old, they are a ticking time bomb in our humid environment. Plan for a refresh before they fail.

3. Verify your backups. Do not just assume they are working. Ask your IT person for a report showing the last successful backup. Better yet, ask them to perform a test restore of a single file to prove it works.

4. Review your security. Ensure every employee is using a unique password and that two-factor authentication is turned on for your most sensitive accounts, especially your email.
