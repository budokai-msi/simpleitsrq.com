## Short answer

Workstation slowdowns, application freezes, and erratic office Wi-Fi cut directly into daily billable hours. 

Before committing to a $1,200 new PC replacement, diagnose the root cause: **90% of office productivity bottlenecks are caused by mechanical hard drive failure, thermal throttling from dust/humidity, or consumer Wi-Fi routers overwhelmed by 15+ connected devices.**

An honest workstation upgrade (e.g., NVMe SSD clone + RAM upgrade) costs **$120-$240 installed**, extending the usable lifespan of a 4-year-old business computer by 3+ years.

## Field note

A accounting firm in downtown Bradenton had three staff members complaining that their PCs took 12 minutes to boot every morning and froze whenever QuickBooks and Excel were open simultaneously.

The owner assumed all three machines needed to be scrapped. Upon inspection, the 4-year-old Dell desktops were powered by 1TB 5400-RPM spinning mechanical hard drives operating at 100% disk usage continuously.

We cloned each drive to a 1TB high-speed NVMe SSD and upgraded the RAM from 8GB to 16GB. Total turnaround time was **under 3 hours per machine**, total hardware cost was under $90 per PC, and boot times dropped from **12 minutes to 11 seconds**.

## 5-Point Office Hardware Checklist This Week

1. **Check Disk Health**: Run `wmic diskdrive get status` in Windows Command Prompt to check for imminent S.M.A.R.T. drive failure.
2. **Inspect UPS Batteries**: Ensure battery backup units are not beeping or flashing red battery replace indicators.
3. **Verify Windows 11 Compatibility**: Run the Microsoft PC Health Check tool on all Windows 10 workstations before support ends.
4. **Clean Dust Filters**: Vacuum front air intake grills on floor-standing workstation towers.
5. **Separate Guest Wi-Fi**: Confirm that office visitors cannot see internal network printers, NAS storage, or server shares.
